Account Registration & Renewal

Before an Employee can begin work, an official account must be registered and activated. The registration includes providing required details, submitting documents, and completing the payment as defined by the Company. Once completed, the Employee will be provided with a login dashboard, a wallet account, and unique credentials.

The account remains valid for 11 months from the date of activation. Upon completion, the Employee must renew the account to continue working. Renewal requires the following:

  • Submitting a renewal request from the dashboard before expiry.
  • Paying the renewal fee as applicable.
  • Agreeing to any updated Terms & Conditions.
  • Company review and approval of the renewal request.

Work Rules

Work assignments are structured into slots, where each slot contains 2000 forms. The Employee must complete all forms within the deadline while maintaining high accuracy. Forms must be completed individually without unauthorized automation. Any detected malpractice shall lead to deductions or termination.

Payment Terms & Conditions

Payments are calculated based on the accuracy of submitted forms. Correct forms are paid at ₹10 per form, with deductions applied for errors as per the defined error structure. Payments undergo a verification process within 7–15 working days. After verification, amounts are credited weekly into the Employee’s wallet on every Sunday. Withdrawals can be made to the registered bank account, subject to a minimum balance of ₹500 in the wallet. The Company is not responsible for any bank charges or delays incurred during the transfer process.

Employees can withdraw their earnings once the wallet balance reaches a minimum of ₹1000. Withdrawal is done directly to the registered bank account.
